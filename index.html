Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should mulch be in garden beds?

A depth of 3 inches is optimal for weed prevention and soil moisture retention. Avoid piling mulch higher than 4 inches, and never pile mulch against tree trunks (known as a 'mulch volcano') as it rots the tree bark.

How many bags of mulch equal one cubic yard?

One cubic yard contains 27 cubic feet. Therefore, 1 cubic yard equals 13.5 bags of 2.0 cu ft mulch (round up to 14) or 18 bags of 1.5 cu ft mulch.

How often do I need to replace mulch?

Organic bark and hardwood mulch decompose and should be refreshed annually with a 1-to-2-inch top layer. Cedar and cypress last slightly longer (up to 2 years) due to natural rot-resistant oils.
